    What you can say

    What you say is recognised by Google's speech service, the one Chrome uses for voice typing. Paused, it still listens, but only for “resume”. The Microphone off button, or closing this panel, turns the microphone off completely.

    Chrome is about to ask whether AccessiFlow can use the microphone. Choose Allow.
