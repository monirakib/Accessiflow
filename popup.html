On every site, name the buttons your screen reader would otherwise call just “button”. The button’s class names and nearby text are sent to the helper service, never anything you type. Form summaries are then written by Smart help too; without this they are made on your computer from the form’s labels.

The page summary is on the Home screen, and Alt+Shift+B reads it out on any page.

What happens to my page?

When you press one of these buttons, AccessiFlow sends that picture or that page text to its helper service, which writes the answer and sends it back. The service keeps nothing.

AccessiFlow remembers each answer on your own computer for 30 days, so a page you come back to is fixed instantly and nothing is sent twice. “Forget the remembered answers” clears them.

Nothing is sent without a button press unless you turn on “Fix unlabelled buttons automatically”. Even then, AccessiFlow makes at most 40 automatic requests an hour, and never sends anything you have typed into a form. Rewritten text is always marked as rewritten, with the original one press away.
