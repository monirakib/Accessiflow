    What gets sent to the internet

    Almost everything AccessiFlow does happens on your own computer. Smart help is the exception: when you press one of its buttons, such as Summarise, the page text or a picture goes to the AccessiFlow helper service, which writes the answer and sends it back. Nothing is stored there and nothing is linked to you.

    Nothing is sent without a button press unless you turn on automatic fixes in Smart help, and never anything you type into a form.

    If something goes wrong
    The panel opens but the page does not change.
    Browsers do not let extensions change their own pages, such as settings or the extension store. Try an ordinary website.
    A page looks wrong after AccessiFlow changed it.
    Press Alt+Shift+8, or turn off “On for this site” in the panel. That site goes back to normal and every other site keeps your setup.
    A shortcut does nothing.
    Another program may use the same keys. You can change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

    AccessiFlow follows WCAG 2.2 at levels AA and AAA, and is built to that standard itself. If something here is hard to use, that is a fault worth reporting.
